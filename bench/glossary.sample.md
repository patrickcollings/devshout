## Terms
- !**CORSIA** — Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation
  - heard: core sea, corsair, causia, cor see ah
- !**My Supply** — Abatable product: a buyer's supply of credits
  - heard: my supplies, mice apply, my supplier
- !**NDC** — Nationally Determined Contribution
  - heard: N D C, endy see, and see
- **proponent** — the developer of a carbon project
  - heard: proponents, component
- **procurement** — buying carbon credits on a buyer's behalf
- **PDD** — Project Design Document
  - heard: P D D, pee dee dee
- **Article 6** — the Paris Agreement article on international carbon markets
  - heard: article six
- **corresponding adjustment** — an accounting adjustment so a credit is not counted twice by two countries
- **vintage** — the year the emission reductions happened
- **registry** — the body that issues and tracks credits
- **Verra** — a carbon credit registry
  - heard: Vera, Verra's
- **Gold Standard** — a carbon credit registry
- **buffer pool** — credits held back by a registry to cover the risk of reversal
- **ARR** — Afforestation, Reforestation and Revegetation
  - heard: A R R, are are
- **ERPA** — Emission Reductions Purchase Agreement
  - heard: E R P A, Erpa, erpah
- **UNFCCC** — United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change
  - heard: U N F triple C, un fcc
- **Supabase** — the hosted Postgres database and backend we use
  - heard: super base, supa base
- **retirements** — credits permanently used up by a buyer
- **In Progress** — a Linear status
- **pnpm** — the package manager
  - heard: P N P M, pee en pee em
- **SupplyTable.tsx** — the My Supply table component
  - heard: supply table dot t s x
